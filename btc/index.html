What this does not tell you

Difficulty only goes one way over time. This calculator holds it flat at today's value, which flatters every forward-looking number on the page. Payback periods in particular are optimistic by construction — a rig that pays back in eight months on flat difficulty rarely pays back in eight months.

The halving row assumes nothing else moves. Price and difficulty both react to a halving. Treat that number as the mechanical effect of the subsidy cut alone, not a forecast.

Fees are an average, not a promise. Average reward per block comes from recent blocks. Fee revenue is lumpy and a quiet week looks nothing like a busy one.

Uptime is assumed at 100%. Real deployments lose hours to curtailment, heat, firmware and the grid. Derate the result by whatever uptime your contract actually delivers.
