# NJS Fitness: the philosophy

Written 2026-11-24 from what Jay has said across the fitness sessions. Read this before writing fitness copy,
changing the fitness nav, or adding a fitness page. It is a draft of Jay's thinking as best understood: Jay
corrects it, and his corrections win.

## The one line

**I train you, and I give you the tools to keep learning without me.**

A coach who only sells sessions keeps you dependent. This site does both jobs: it books the sessions, and it
hands over what a client needs on the days Jay is not in the room.

## The three home gyms

Jay coaches at three gyms, and they are the three corners of the map:

- UFC Gym Brea (North OC)
- 9Round Rancho Santa Margarita (South OC)
- Club Studio Corona (Inland Empire)

Train with him at the one nearest you. Everything else on the site sits around those three points.

## The well-rounded athlete: five pillars

Strength, engine, mobility, control, skill (`partials/fitness/path.json`, `/fitness/path`). No single gym and no
single coach covers all five well. The site says so, and points to what fills the gap:

| You train with Jay | Add nearby | It adds |
|---|---|---|
| Any home gym | A yoga studio | Mobility |
| Any home gym | A reformer Pilates studio | Control |
| Any home gym | A run or interval studio | Engine |
| Any home gym | A boxing, kickboxing or Muay Thai gym | Skill |

That is why the near-me map measures from a home gym by default, and why every card says what it adds.

## The tools, and what each is for

| Tool | Where | The job |
|---|---|---|
| Sessions | `/fitness/book`, the 12-pack | Jay coaches you, one on one |
| The path | `/fitness/path`, focus pages | Shows what getting better looks like, level by level |
| The map | `/fitness/guides/near-me` | Finds the studio that adds what you are missing, near where you train |
| Gym guides | `/fitness/guides/...` | What each gym is known for, from sources, in Jay's order once he ranks them |
| Coach's notes | `/fitness/notes/...` | Warm-ups, band work, recovery: what to do alone, with sources |

## How it must sound

- Generous, not salesy. Sending a client to a yoga studio Jay earns nothing from is the point: it shows he is
  after the result, not the hours.
- A working coach, first person, plain words. "I", never "we".
- Honest. A place is "close by" until Jay has been there; then it can be "I recommend". A guide is a list until
  he ranks every gym on it; then it is a ranking. No copied star ratings, no invented scarcity, no health claim
  without a source that was read.
- Chains are shown, never ranked and never given the laurel.

## What this means for design

- One colour and one emblem per kind, everywhere: coral striking, teal Pilates, violet yoga, amber run and HIIT.
- The three home gyms always stand out from every other pin.
- Every page should answer two questions: what do I do with Jay, and what do I do on my own?

## Open questions for Jay

2. His rank order for each guide, so the laurel can say "No. 1 in my boxing ranking".
3. A sentence in his own words on why he sends clients elsewhere. It belongs on the coach page.
