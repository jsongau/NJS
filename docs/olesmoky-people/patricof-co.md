What the structure tells you is unambiguous. An athlete investment platform buys into a consumer brand because its members bring reach and cultural association. The value they add is distribution of attention, not operating input.

## How they would read a loyalty and CRM work sample

They would not read it closely, and treating them as a decision maker would be a mistake. Their relevance is indirect and worth exactly one line in any pitch: an athlete investor base is an owned acquisition channel that most spirits brands have to rent. A loyalty programme with a referral mechanic and a measurable cost per acquired member gives that channel something to point at, and gives the company a way to measure what an athlete post is actually worth beyond impressions.

The connection to the new CEO is the interesting part. Michael Novy came from Calabasas Beverage Company, where the growth engine was celebrity association through Kendall Jenner's 818 and Kylie Jenner's Sprinter. Ole Smoky already holds an athlete investor roster and a University of Tennessee Athletics partnership as the exclusive moonshine of the Vols, announced August 2024. A CRM work sample that shows how identified members turn a celebrity or athlete moment into captured, consented records rather than a spike in traffic that leaves nothing behind is speaking directly to the pattern Novy has spent the last several years running.
