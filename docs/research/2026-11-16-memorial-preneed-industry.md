## 1. Refuge Memorial, the company

Source: refugememorial.com, read 2026-11-16.

- **What it is:** "a 100% virtual funeral preplanning company" in Rancho Cucamonga, CA. It says it
  works nationwide and independently, and that it does not work for funeral homes.
- **Tagline:** "A better way to preplan." Homepage headline: "Plan ahead. Protect your family. On your terms."
- **Services:** funeral pre-planning (casket or urn, service preferences) usable with "any provider
  nationwide"; help documenting VA burial benefits (cemetery space, headstones, flags); simple
  cremation plans (merchandise, documented wishes, funding). An "Estate" link goes to a separate subdomain.
- **Pricing:** "Prices based on national averages for flexibility." "Pay in full or at your own pace."
  "Can payoff at any time." No prices listed.
- **Channels seen:** Instagram, Facebook, testimonials. No physical locations; the process is "entirely virtual."
- **The job (from the posting Jay shared):** Meta Ads/CRM Specialist, GoHighLevel, Meta, Zapier,
  Rancho Cucamonga, about $40/hr.

### What that means for the build
1. **The product is a plan plus its funding, sold online.** The funnel's job is to move a stranger
   from an ad to a signed, funded plan without a funeral home visit. That means a plan builder,
   a price, a payment plan, and a licensed person on a call.
2. **Portability is the selling point.** "Use with any provider nationwide" answers the fear that a
   prepaid plan dies if you move or the funeral home is sold (the California Bureau lists exactly
   this as a question to ask; see section 4).
3. **"Pay at your own pace" almost certainly means insurance-funded pre-need** (**verify** with
   Refuge). If the ads mention payment plans or insurance, they fall under Meta's Financial products
   and services special ad category (section 7), which removes age targeting. That is the single
   biggest constraint on the Meta account and the best interview point.
4. **Veterans are a named segment.** A veteran mode, a "did they serve?" field and a DD-214 step are
   not decoration; they map to a service Refuge already sells.
5. **"Estate" is the adjacent product.** Wills, trusts, advance directives and beneficiary updates
   are the natural next conversation after a funeral plan (section 6).

- Cremation is the majority and still rising. Plans, ads and widgets should lead with cremation

  ten-minute online step is the right product shape.
- Pre-need is where the large operator is growing fastest (SCI +11% in Q4 2025).

## 3. Ways to pay for a funeral in advance

| Option | Who gets the money | Price locked? | Flexible for the family? | Health questions | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Pre-need insurance | The funeral provider named in the contract | Usually yes | No: pays for the arranged services | Usually light | Paid in full or over time (TruStage) |
| Final expense (burial) life insurance | A named beneficiary, in cash | No | Yes: any bill | Simplified issue: a few questions; guaranteed issue: none, costs more | Typically 1,000 to 25,000 dollars, ages 45+; many have a 2-year waiting period (NerdWallet) |
| Pre-need trust (California) | Held in trust for the contract | Depends on contract | No | None | Cancel for a refund including interest; the seller may keep up to 10% of the original contract, only from interest (CA Bureau consumer guide) |
| Payable-on-death bank account | Named beneficiary | No | Yes | None | "The cost ... is usually not guaranteed" (CA Bureau) |
| Pay at the time | — | No | — | — | Family pays from savings, the estate, or benefits |

### Final expense insurance, sample monthly premiums
Guaranteed issue whole life, average of the three lowest rates per age, from Choice Mutual via
NerdWallet, as of 2026-09-10. Sample only, not a quote.

| Age | Male 10k | Male 15k | Male 20k | Female 10k | Female 15k | Female 20k |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 50 | 40 | 60 | 79 | 30 | 45 | 60 |
| 60 | 57 | 85 | 113 | 43 | 64 | 84 |
| 70 | 87 | 130 | 172 | 64 | 95 | 127 |
| 80 | 157 | 235 | 313 | 127 | 190 | 253 |

**Waiting period:** if the insured dies of natural causes in the first two (sometimes three) years,
beneficiaries usually get a refund of premiums or a reduced payout, not the face amount (NerdWallet).

### Copy rules for this category
- Selling or advising on insurance needs a license. Pages say "talk to a licensed agent"; calculators
  say "estimate, not a quote".
- Never promise a price is locked unless the contract guarantees it. The CA Bureau tells consumers to
  ask exactly this.

## 4. California rules that shape the funnel

- **Price list online.** A funeral establishment with a website must post the goods and services on
  its general price list, linked from the homepage, using a word like "goods", "merchandise",
  "products" or "services", or a "price information" link (B&P Code 7685). A virtual pre-planner is
  not a funeral establishment (**verify** how Refuge is licensed), but the transparency expectation carries over.
- **Questions the Bureau tells consumers to ask** before a pre-need contract (cfb.ca.gov, pre-need page):
  are prices guaranteed; what is excluded; does the plan transfer if you move; what happens if the
  provider closes or is sold; who holds the money; who pays tax on interest; what the cancellation
  penalty is. **Every one of these is an FAQ item and an objection the funnel should answer up front.**
- **License check:** Cemetery and Funeral Bureau, (916) 574-7870.
- **FTC Funeral Rule:** itemized price lists on request and no required packages. The FTC has been
  working on an update that would require online price posting; status as of 2026 not confirmed (**verify**).

## 6. Estate planning: the adjacent product

| Item | What it does | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Will | Names who inherits and a guardian for minors | — |
| Revocable living trust | Passes assets without probate | — |
| Advance Health Care Directive (California) | Names an agent for health decisions if you cannot speak for yourself | Family Caregiver Alliance |
| POLST | A doctor's order with treatment choices (comfort only, limited, full) that follows the patient | same |
| Durable power of attorney for finances | An agent for money matters (the health agent cannot manage finances) | same |
| Beneficiary designations | Life insurance, retirement accounts and POD accounts pass outside the will | — |

- **California small estate limit:** 208,850 dollars gross value for deaths on or after April 1, 2025;
  under it, heirs can use the small estate affidavit instead of probate (Sacramento County Public Law Library).
  The affidavit has a 40-day wait after death (**verify** Probate Code 13100).
- **Statutory probate attorney fee** (Probate Code 10810): 4% of the first 100,000 dollars, 3% of
  the next 100,000, 2% of the next 800,000, 1% of the next 9 million, 0.5% of the next 15 million,
  court-set above 25 million, on the gross estate. The executor's statutory fee (Probate Code 10800)
  uses the same schedule (**verify**). Example: a 600,000-dollar home with a mortgage still counts
  at 600,000, so each fee is 15,000 dollars.
- **Medi-Cal asset limit:** 130,000 dollars in 2026, cut to 21,000 for seniors and people with
  disabilities from July 2027 (CalMatters, Oct 2026). Burial plots count under the new cap per that
  article. Prepaid funeral plans are often excluded from Medicaid assets, with limits that vary by
  state (Caring.com) (**verify** California's current rule with DHCS). This makes "plan and fund it
  before July 2027" a real, dated, honest reason to act now for families near Medi-Cal, if DHCS
  confirms the exclusion.

## 7. Advertising this category on Meta

- **Financial products and services special ad category** (Jon Loomer): replaced the Credit category
  and includes insurance. Required in the U.S. from early 2025. Under it you **cannot target by age
  or gender, cannot target by ZIP code** (locations must cover at least a 15-mile radius), some
  detailed targeting is unavailable, and **lookalike audiences are not allowed**.
- So a pre-need ad that mentions insurance or payment plans cannot be aimed at people 55+. The
  audience has to come from **the creative and the offer** (images and words that speak to people
  planning for themselves or a parent), from first-party lists in the CRM, and from retargeting
  site visitors (**verify** what custom audience use is still allowed in the category).
- **Personal attributes policy:** ad copy cannot state or imply a viewer's age, health or loss.
- **Ad copy that works inside both rules:** speak about the plan, not the person ("A funeral plan
  you can set up from home, honored by any provider"), never "Are you over 60?" or "Did you just lose…".
- **Tracking:** with demographic targeting gone, conversion data is the steering wheel. Send booked
  calls and signed plans back to Meta (Conversions API, via GoHighLevel or Zapier), not just form fills.

## 9. Messaging that moves people to act, without fake urgency (added 2026-11-16)

Jay asked for FOMO messaging per field. Rule kept: no invented scarcity, countdowns or deadlines
(CLAUDE.md honest-copy rule; the FTC treats false scarcity and fake timers as dark patterns, see
the 2022 staff report). The honest version of FOMO is loss aversion grounded in a true, dated fact:
show what the person loses by waiting, and cite it.

| Field | True reason to act now | Line that uses it | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Final expense insurance | Premiums are priced by age at purchase, and most simplified or guaranteed policies have a 2-year waiting period that only starts on the day you buy | "The two-year wait starts the day you sign up, not the day you need it." | NerdWallet funeral insurance |
| Final expense insurance | Health questions: qualifying is easier while healthy; later, only guaranteed-issue (costs more) may be left | "Answer the health questions while the answers are easy." | NerdWallet |
| Pre-need plan | Locks today's price for the goods and services in the contract. Caution: NFDA found funeral medians rose slower than inflation 2021 to 2023 (burial +5.8%, cremation +8.1%, inflation 13.6%), so do not claim prices are "soaring" | "Lock in today's price list for the choices you make." | NFDA 2023 GPL study via Funeral Director Daily |
| Pre-need plan, family angle | Families decide in about a day, under grief, without the wishes | "Every choice you make now is one your family won't have to guess at." | Industry norm; no stat needed |
| Estate planning | 24% of adults have a will (Caring.com 2025); without one, California probate fees run by statute on the gross estate | "Three in four adults have no will. Fifteen minutes puts you in the other group." | Caring.com; Probate Code 10810 |
| Veterans | VA burial allowance and honors are real money families miss; the amounts change every October 1 | "If they served, the VA may cover burial and a headstone. Most families never ask." | VA, research section 5 |
| Senior care / Medi-Cal | The asset limit returned Jan 1, 2026 at 130,000 dollars (Justice in Aging). A further cut to 21,000 from July 2027 is reported by CalMatters but **verify with DHCS** before using it. Whether funeral trusts are exempt is **not confirmed** | Only after DHCS confirms: "Medi-Cal rules change in July 2027. See what counts before then." | Justice in Aging FAQ; CalMatters Oct 2026 |
| Senior care | Tours and assessment take weeks; families who start early choose, families who start in a crisis take what is open | "Tour while it's a choice, not a scramble." | Plain-language claim, no stat |

Never use for any field: countdown timers, "only N spots left", "price goes up Friday" unless true
and dated, anything aimed at the bereaved (at-need visitors never see urgency), or copy that implies
the viewer's age, health or loss (Meta personal attributes policy).

Sources: [FTC dark patterns staff report summary (Venable)](https://www.venable.com/insights/blogs/2022/09/the-ftc-brings-more-light-to-dark-patterns-in-new),
[NFDA 2023 cost vs inflation](https://funeraldirectordaily.com/?p=20091),

## 10. What memorial, cremation, insurance and senior care advertisers run (added 2026-11-16)

### Landing page structure that recurs
Situation picker, then headline plus one subhead, the price or a price-list link, three or four steps, the free
guide or a call, trust lines (license number, years, real reviews), a service area, FAQ, and a phone number on
every screen (Tulip, Neptune, Dignity Memorial).

### Never in the builder
Fear or guilt hooks, countdowns, "everyone is approved", "1 dollar a month" style claims, a stated or implied
age, health or loss of the viewer, testimonials without a disclosure, and any real brand's name or look.
