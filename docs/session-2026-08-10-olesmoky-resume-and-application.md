## The correction that matters

Earlier in the session the analysis treated 1.2M nine litre cases in 2024 as growth
from 1M in 2021. It is not. Per `docs/olesmoky-evidence-register.md`, 2024 volume was
**down 6% year on year**, footfall fell from roughly 5.7M in 2021 to 5.0M plus in 2023,
and the company stopped publishing after that. Michael Novy was brought in as CEO in
August 2025 over exactly this.

Consequences. The pitch is not "you have traffic you are not capturing," it is
"traffic and volume are both falling and the only asset nobody has built yet is the
customer file." A first year CEO out of 818 Tequila and Sprinter, carrying a board
mandate to move, is the most likely person in the company to open an unsolicited
working prototype. Direct outreach odds are better than the session first estimated.
Compensation reads slightly tighter, not looser.

## Where the resume ended up

The through line changed from "builder who does CRM" to "CRM and lifecycle operator
who can build." That reordering did more than any individual rewrite. KYT now opens on
Lifecycle Strategy and closes on Loyalty and Merchandising, so the first thing a
recruiter reads is program ownership rather than a platform name.

Coverage against the eleven posted responsibilities went from nine to eleven in one
pass, with no new claims, by using facts already on file:

- **Field and experiential capture** was invisible. Restored the KYT grassroots
  community events that fed contacts into the CRM. This had been cut for space and
  should not be cut again on any consumer brand application.
- **On pack and POS anonymous to known** was invisible. Reframed the visit driven
  journeys as converting in person traffic into known, opted in contacts at the point
  of service. True, and near verbatim to their bullet 8.

Gloria Jean's came back after being cut, for two reasons that only surfaced when the
posting was read against the preferred experience line rather than the requirements
list. It is the only **hospitality** on the page, and it carried a **store level
customer rewards program**, which is the third loyalty data point and the closest
structural analog on the resume to the Hooch Hop. Canyon Coach Lines stayed cut.

Other changes: dropped "Selected GoHighLevel" so the reader meets the capability before
the tool; retired "First-Party Data" as a heading in favour of Customer Data
Architecture; Tawa's Instacart bullet became Delivery and Omnichannel Commerce; skills
reordered so capability precedes software.

## The diagnostic worth keeping

Jay's numbers are numerators. $30K on $1K, 700+ reviews, 20,000+ subscribers, 3,000+
SKUs. Real, verifiable, no scale attached. Director candidates win on denominators:
2.1M members, 4.2M resolved profiles, $3M budget, six reports. No rewrite converts one
into the other.

The documents moved from roughly 3/10 to 8/10 across this session and the odds moved
about two points, because the gap was scale rather than wording. Check which one you
are facing before spending hours on a document.

Ranked value of the remaining actions on this application: emailing the decision maker
directly, roughly eight points. Adding the responsible drinking paragraph to the cover
letter, one to two. Building a LinkedIn profile, one here and it unlocks sourcing
generally. Klaviyo hands on, zero here and five to eight on every future application.
Further resume editing, approximately zero.
