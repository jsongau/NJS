A session covers

Hands, feet,and the setup.

New to boxing? We start with stance and the jab. Trained before? We sharpen what you have and add the setups.

Focus mitts

I call combinations and catch them. You learn to throw with snap, then with power.

Speed paddles

Fast, light targets that sharpen your reaction time and hand speed.

Footwork

Step, pivot and cut angles without giving up your base.

Setups and combinations

Jab to open, then the right hand or the hook you set up. One, two, three, built up.

Defense

Slips, rolls and blocks, so you hit without getting hit.

Rounds to finish

Timed rounds on the mitts, on the bag or shadowboxing.

Stance

Orthodox, southpaw,or switch.

Your stance decides which hand jabs and which side carries the power. We drill both sides, so switching becomes a choice you make on purpose.

Left foot forward

You jab with your left and throw power with your right. Where most right-handed people start.

Right foot forward

The mirror image: jab with your right, power with your left. Natural for most lefties, and awkward for orthodox opponents to face.

Change stance on purpose

Step through to the other side to open new angles or load the rear leg for a harder kick. We build it slowly so your balance comes with you.
