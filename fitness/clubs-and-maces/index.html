Fitness / Clubs and maces

Rare specialty

Steel clubs and maces.Shoulders built to last.

The shield cast, the mill, the reverse mill and the mace 360. Swinging an offset weight trains your shoulders, grip and core through ranges most gym work never reaches. Most gyms don't teach it. I do.

The movements

Four swingsI'll teach you.

Each one is learned light and slow, then built up. Technique first, always.

Steel club

Shield cast

The club starts upright at your chest, drops behind one shoulder and swings like a pendulum, then you pull it back up through the elbow. Same shoulder for the whole set.

Steel club

Mill

Two circles in one: a small one near your head, driven by the elbow, and a big cast across the front of your body that comes back behind your back. The elbow circles inward.

Steel club

Reverse mill

The same pattern with the elbow circling outward, like a jab, and the cast going out to the side. It loads the shoulder in the opposite direction.

Steel mace

Mace 360

Both hands on the handle. The ball drops behind your back like a pendulum, comes over one shoulder and down in front. Hips stay braced while your shoulders, lats and core steer it.

What it builds

Strong, mobile,durable shoulders.

Clubs and maces train the shoulder through full circles under load, not just up and down. That's why I pair them with strength work in almost every plan.

01

Shoulder strength through every angle

Circles and casts load the shoulder overhead, behind the head and across the body, where most lifting doesn't go.

02

Grip and forearms

Holding a weight that's trying to pull away from you builds a stronger grip with every rep.

03

Shoulders that hold up

This is how I train my own shoulders for years of lifting and striking: controlled load through the full circle, built up slowly. Research on clubs and maces is still small; what it shows so far is stronger grip and better short-term shoulder mobility.

New to it, or coming back from a shoulder injury? We start light, keep every swing controlled, and build from there. Check with your doctor or physio first if your shoulder is still being treated.
