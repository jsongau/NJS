Method

How this listis made.

Last checked November 23, 2026.

01

Facts come from sources.

What a gym is known for comes from its own site, news coverage or a published directory, all listed below. The list is grouped by region, not ranked: I add my own order as I get to know each gym.

02

No paid placements.

No gym paid or asked to be here. I do not copy star ratings; follow the link and read the reviews yourself.

03

Where I coach.

I teach at UFC Gym Brea, 9Round in Rancho Santa Margarita and Club Studio in Corona, so I left them off this list. See where I train clients.

Sources: Each studio's own site and booking listing, linked on its profile. A gym missing or changed? Tell me.
