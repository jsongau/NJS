Plan your first session

Learn to hit, and hit well.

  1. Stance and guard. Feet, hands and how to move without losing balance.
  2. Rounds on pads. I hold mitts and Thai pads while you put the first combos together.
  3. Your next four weeks. What we work on next, and what to drill on your own.

Move better and breathe easier.

  1. A movement check. Where you're tight, where you're strong, what hurts.
  2. A flow built for you. Paced to how your body feels that day.
  3. Poses to keep. A short practice you can do at home between sessions.

Build a strong, steady core.

  1. Your reformer setup. Springs, straps and footbar set for your body.
  2. Core and alignment. The basics every later session builds on.
  3. A plan to progress. How we add load as you get stronger.

What I coach

Strike. Flow. Core.

Three disciplines that cover each other's gaps: power, range of motion and the control in between.

01

Strike

Kickboxing

Fundamentals coached on focus mitts, speed paddles and Thai pads. Footwork, combinations and defense, built for real conditioning.

02

Flow

Yoga

More than 400 hours of yoga. Mobility, breath and recovery, so the hard days don't pile up in your back and hips.

03

Core

Reformer Pilates

Over a year on the reformer. Slow, exact strength for the deep muscles that keep you moving well and out of the injury list.

How it works

Assess, plan, train, track.

The same way I build software: start with what's true, change one thing at a time, and measure it.

  1. 01

    Assess

    A first session to see how you move and hear what you want. No sales pitch.

  2. 02

    Plan

    A week that fits your schedule: classes, private sessions, and what to do on your own.

  3. 03

    Train

    With me at the gym or one on one. Form first, every time.

  4. 04

    Track

    Sessions, streaks and progress in my coaching app, so you see it working. App coming soon.
