The art of eight limbs

Fists, elbows,knees and shins.

Muay Thai adds knees, elbows and the clinch to kickboxing. Each one is taught on pads, light and controlled, before we add power.

Teep

The Muay Thai front kick. A jab with your leg, to keep range and stop pressure.

Round kick

The shin kick Muay Thai is famous for, thrown through the target.

Knees

Straight knees driven up through the hips, from range and in the clinch.

Elbows

Short, sharp strikes from close range. Taught on pads, with control.

Clinch

Hand position, posture and balance when you're chest to chest.

    Stance

    Orthodox, southpaw,or switch.

    Your stance decides which hand jabs and which side carries the power. We drill both sides, so switching becomes a choice you make on purpose.

    Left foot forward

    You jab with your left and throw power with your right. Where most right-handed people start.

    Right foot forward

    The mirror image: jab with your right, power with your left. Natural for most lefties, and awkward for orthodox opponents to face.

    Change stance on purpose

    Step through to the other side to open new angles or load the rear leg for a harder kick. We build it slowly so your balance comes with you.
