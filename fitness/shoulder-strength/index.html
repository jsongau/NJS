The 12-pack, week by week

Six weeks,three phases.

  1. Weeks 1 and 2

    A range check and the basic swings with a light club.

  2. Weeks 3 and 4

    Heavier clubs and your first mace work.

  3. Weeks 5 and 6

    Longer sets and pressing strength, checked against week 1.

  4. After week 6

    Keep going with a new 12-pack, or train on your own with a written plan.

Questions

Before you book.

I've never done this. Is that OK?

Yes. Every swing is learned light and slow. If you have a shoulder injury, check with your doctor first.

Can I mix it with something else?

Yes. A 12-pack can carry two focus areas. Muscle building adds pressing strength, and striking uses the shoulders you build.
