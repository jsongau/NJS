Why the scarf comes first

You are not really in until you have a scarf over your head.

My first match I stood there not knowing the songs. Someone handed me a scarf, and by halftime I was singing. So I made a scarf worth handing to a first-timer, then built the rest of the bag around it.

This store is what actually rides in my matchday bag, nothing I would not hand a first-timer.

The honest part

I earn from this edit, and I would rather you know.

As an LA Galaxy creator affiliate I earn 5% of every order here, before tax and shipping. Your 10% with AVA10 comes off your side, never added to your price. My interest is you actually coming to matches, so I only sell what earns its place in the bag.
