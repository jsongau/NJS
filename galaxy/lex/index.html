Why the flag came first

You watch a match. In the section, you are the match.

I have stood in the north end for eleven seasons, and the flags I could find were either too small to read or fell apart by August. So I made one that is oversized, double-sided, and built to be waved all match, then built the rest of the kit around it.

This store is what actually rides in my matchday bag, nothing I would not hand someone joining the section.

The honest part

I earn from this edit, and I would rather you know.

As an LA Galaxy creator affiliate I earn 5% of every order here, before tax and shipping. Your 10% with LEX10 comes off your side, never added to your price. My interest is you actually in the stands, so I only sell what earns its place in the kit.

New to the section? The scarf and a single ticket are all you need for your first match.
