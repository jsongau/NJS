What happens after they enter

Winning isn't the campaign. What happens next is.

The sweepstakes creates the first interaction. The customer journey creates the relationship. Every entrant follows an automated lifecycle, branched on the plate they picked, built to turn one entry into repeat visits, gift card purchases, referrals, and future catering orders.

Day 0
Welcome and bonus offer

The plate match, the $30 code, and the ordering link arrive while interest is at its highest.

Day 2
Best seller spotlight

Their menu pick, reinforced with best-seller imagery and social proof that builds the confidence to visit.

Day 5
Limited time reminder

A friendly nudge before the $30 code expires. One simple call to action, one clear link.

Day 9
Personalized next offer

Family Meals, catering, or the next right offer, chosen from the household signal in their quiz answers.

Day 14
Win them back

One final reminder before the code expires, then the entrant moves into re-engagement for seasonal promotions and limited-time offers.

Unused, dormant and never redeemed

Aging

California Civil Code 1749.5 bars expiration dates and dormancy fees on gift certificates, and balances under $15 must be redeemable in cash. Unused value is therefore an obligation that stays on the books, not money the business has won.

Official rules summary

This is a concept work sample. The summary below shows the structure a real sweepstakes needs, and would require legal review before any launch.

Sponsor
Would be named here, with mailing address.
Eligibility
Legal residents of California and Arizona, 18 years or older. Employees of the sponsor and their immediate households are excluded.
No purchase necessary
A purchase does not improve the chance of winning. A free mail-in entry method would be published here.
Entry period
Fixed start and end date and time, stated in a single time zone.
Prize
Three $200 Ono Hawaiian BBQ gift cards are awarded each monthly drawing period. Approximate retail value $200 each, $600 per period. Winners are responsible for any applicable taxes.
Odds
Depend on the number of eligible entries received.
Winner selection
Three winners drawn at random from all eligible entries each period using an auditable process, with an alternate-winner procedure.
Notification
By email within a stated number of days, with a claim deadline and publicity release.
Referral entries
One additional entry per verified friend who completes an entry, capped at three. Duplicate, automated, or invalid entries are void.
Privacy
Email and SMS permission are collected separately. Data use, retention, and opt-out are disclosed at the point of entry.
Platform disclosure
The promotion is not sponsored, endorsed, or administered by any social platform.
