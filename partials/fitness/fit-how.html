How it works

Assess, plan, train, track.

The same way I build software: start with what's true, change one thing at a time, and measure it.

  1. 01

    Assess

    A first session to see how you move and hear what you want. No sales pitch.

  2. 02

    Plan

    A week that fits your schedule: classes, private sessions, and what to do on your own.

  3. 03

    Train

    With me at the gym or one on one. Form first, every time.

  4. 04

    Track

    Sessions, streaks and progress in my coaching app, so you see it working. App coming soon.
