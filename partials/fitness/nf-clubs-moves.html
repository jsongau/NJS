The movements

Four swingsI'll teach you.

Each one is learned light and slow, then built up. Technique first, always.

Steel club

Shield cast

The club starts upright at your chest, drops behind one shoulder and swings like a pendulum, then you pull it back up through the elbow. Same shoulder for the whole set.

Steel club

Mill

Two circles in one: a small one near your head, driven by the elbow, and a big cast across the front of your body that comes back behind your back. The elbow circles inward.

Steel club

Reverse mill

The same pattern with the elbow circling outward, like a jab, and the cast going out to the side. It loads the shoulder in the opposite direction.

Steel mace

Mace 360

Both hands on the handle. The ball drops behind your back like a pendulum, comes over one shoulder and down in front. Hips stay braced while your shoulders, lats and core steer it.
