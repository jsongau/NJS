Stance

Orthodox, southpaw,or switch.

Your stance decides which hand jabs and which side carries the power. We drill both sides, so switching becomes a choice you make on purpose.

Left foot forward

You jab with your left and throw power with your right. Where most right-handed people start.

Right foot forward

The mirror image: jab with your right, power with your left. Natural for most lefties, and awkward for orthodox opponents to face.

Change stance on purpose

Step through to the other side to open new angles or load the rear leg for a harder kick. We build it slowly so your balance comes with you.
