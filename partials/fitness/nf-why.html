Why twice a week

Discipline is built,one week at a time.

01

Once a week is a visit. Twice is a routine.

Two fixed days give your week a shape. Training stops being a decision you make every time.

02

Progress sticks when the gap is short.

Three or four days between sessions is short enough that you start where you left off, not from scratch.

03

Six weeks is long enough to see it.

Lifts go up, clothes fit differently, and you notice the change yourself.
