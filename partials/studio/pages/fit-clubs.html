What it builds

Strong, mobile,durable shoulders.

Clubs and maces train the shoulder through full circles under load, not just up and down. That's why I pair them with strength work in almost every plan.

01

Shoulder strength through every angle

Circles and casts load the shoulder overhead, behind the head and across the body, where most lifting doesn't go.

02

Grip and forearms

Holding a weight that's trying to pull away from you builds a stronger grip with every rep.

03

Shoulders that hold up

This is how I train my own shoulders for years of lifting and striking: controlled load through the full circle, built up slowly. Research on clubs and maces is still small; what it shows so far is stronger grip and better short-term shoulder mobility.

New to it, or coming back from a shoulder injury? We start light, keep every swing controlled, and build from there. Check with your doctor or physio first if your shoulder is still being treated.
