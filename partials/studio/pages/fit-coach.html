My story

I train it first.Then I teach it.

I'm Nathan J. Song. Some clients know me as Nathan Song, and some as Jiaming Song. I coach kickboxing and boxing, and I train people one on one across Orange County and the Inland Empire.

My base is functional mobility and dynamic striking. I have more than 400 hours of yoga training behind me, I swing steel clubs and maces, and I coach striking on pads, mitts and Thai pads from the first stance to full rounds.

Fat loss and muscle building are where I proved my own method. I won SHRED, a body transformation challenge, three times in a row, and the same habits are in every plan I write.

Today I'm on the gym floor seven days a week, teaching classes at three gyms and coaching privately around them. I'm completing my reformer Pilates certification, because I only teach what I practice.

As your head coach, I set your plan and coach every session. I send you out to explore, and you bring it back to me every week.
