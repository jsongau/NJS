The 12-pack, week by week

Six weeks,three phases.

  1. Weeks 1 and 2

    A baseline: how many rounds you can hold at a steady pace.

  2. Weeks 3 and 4

    More rounds or shorter rests, one change at a time.

  3. Weeks 5 and 6

    Full rounds at pace, then a retest of your baseline.

  4. After week 6

    Keep going with a new 12-pack, or train on your own with a written plan.
