The 12-pack, week by week

Six weeks,three phases.

  1. Weeks 1 and 2

    Movement check, your starting numbers, and the lifts we build on.

  2. Weeks 3 and 4

    Load goes up. More sets on the areas you want to grow.

  3. Weeks 5 and 6

    Push the top sets and test against your week 1 numbers.

  4. After week 6

    Keep going with a new 12-pack, or train on your own with a written plan.
