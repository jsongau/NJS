A sore AC joint.What helps.

The AC joint is where your collarbone meets your shoulder blade. A fall onto the shoulder can separate it. I'm a coach, not a doctor: this note is what the medical sources say, and where training fits once you are cleared.

First, see a doctor

What standardcare looks like.

A doctor grades the injury, often with an X-ray. Do that before anything on this page.

01

Rest, cold and a sling

A sling, cold packs and medication manage the pain in almost all patients.

Then rehabilitation

The shoulder needs rehab to rebuild motion, strength and flexibility, whether or not you have surgery.

03

Most people recover

Most people with this injury, even professional athletes, return to normal function with nonsurgical treatment.

Acupuncture

Where acupuncturereally fits.

Some people find it helps with pain while they recover. Here is what the evidence does and does not show.

01

For shoulder pain: not proven

A Cochrane review of nine trials found there is not enough evidence to say whether acupuncture works for shoulder pain. Any benefit looked short-term.

Cochrane: acupuncture for shoulder pain

02

For some pain: it can help

The NIH reports acupuncture can ease some kinds of pain, such as back, neck and knee pain. It does not list shoulder injuries.

NIH: acupuncture, effectiveness and safety

03

It does not repair the joint

I found no studies showing acupuncture heals an AC joint injury. Treat it as possible pain relief beside your doctor's plan, from a licensed acupuncturist, and never as a reason to put off seeing a doctor.

NIH: acupuncture, effectiveness and safety

Once you are cleared

How I'd bringthe shoulder back.

Only after your doctor or physical therapist says you can load it. We go at the pace the shoulder allows.

  1. Range first

    Easy moving stretches and light club circles to get the shoulder moving through its path again.

  2. Steady before strong

    Band pull-aparts, rows and holds for the muscles around the shoulder blade.

  3. Load, slowly

    Pressing and carrying come back last, with small jumps in weight.

  4. Back to your sport

    Pad work, lifting or whatever you were doing before, a piece at a time.
