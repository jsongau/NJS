A session covers

Rotate and steady,then load.

Good for lifters with stiff shoulders, strikers and desk workers. If you have an injury, check with your doctor first.

01

Circles before weight

Light club swings that open the shoulder and teach the path.

02

Steady under load

Presses, carries and holds that make the joint stable.

03

Club and mace swings

The shield cast, the mill and the mace 360, once the basics are smooth.

The 12-pack, week by week

Six weeks,three phases.

  1. Weeks 1 and 2

    A range check and the basic swings with a light club.

  2. Weeks 3 and 4

    Heavier clubs and your first mace work.

  3. Weeks 5 and 6

    Longer sets and pressing strength, checked against week 1.

  4. After week 6

    Keep going with a new 12-pack, or train on your own with a written plan.
