Questions

Before you book.

Do I need to be a member of the gym?

No. The 12-pack comes with your access: 3 years of membership at the gym in Brea, 1 year at 9Round in Rancho Santa Margarita, or 3 months at any Club Studio.

I've never trained before. Where do I start?

With session one. It is a goal talk and a movement check, then a real workout scaled to where you are.

Can I mix strength, clubs and striking?

Yes. A session is a session. We plan the mix around your goal, so most 12-packs combine strength days with clubs, striking or mobility.

How do I pay?

Nothing is charged on this site. Send a request, I confirm your times by email, and you pay me directly. The 12-pack is $2,400, at any of my three gyms or at your place, where that is $200 a session.
