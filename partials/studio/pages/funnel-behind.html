Meta ads

  • One campaign, an ad set per door, each linking straight to its door on the page
  • Pixel and Conversions API share one event ID, so a lead is counted once
  • Optimized on booked calls and tours, not on clicks or form starts
  • Plans funded by insurance run under Meta's financial products special ad category, which limits targeting

Zapier

  • Connects a page that lives outside the CRM: one Zap, a path per door
  • Phone numbers and names are cleaned before they reach the CRM
  • LeadConnector, GoHighLevel's Zapier app, writes each answer to its own field
  • Failed runs alert by email; Zap history is checked weekly

GoHighLevel

  • A pipeline per door, with stages that match how the team works
  • Workflows branch on consent and stop on a booking, a reply or an opt-out
  • Calendar booking moves the opportunity and sends the booked event to Meta
  • At-need contacts never enter a marketing workflow
