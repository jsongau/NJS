In every funnel build

The page is the part people see.

What sits behind it decides whether a lead becomes a booked call. These come with every funnel I build.

  1. Doors, not one pageEach audience gets its own headline, form length and next step, linked straight from its own ad set.
  2. Intake that maps to the CRMEvery answer lands in one custom field, so staff never retype what a customer already told the website.
  3. Speed-to-lead alertsHot leads page a person by text, with a backup alert if nobody calls back in time.
  4. Follow-up with exitsEmail and text sequences that stop on a booking, a reply or an opt-out, with consent stored for every text.
  5. Tracking to the booked callOne event per step to the ad platform and the CRM, so cost is measured to bookings, not clicks.
  6. A Monday reportOne page for the owner: leads by source, cost per booking, what stalled, and one change for the week.
