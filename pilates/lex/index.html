Why the weights came first

Bodyweight builds control. Load builds change.

I coach six days a week, and I kept telling members to add a little load. The ankle weights I could find either slid down mid-set or bruised the bone. So I made a pair that is soft, secure, and heavy enough to matter, then built the rest of the kit around it.

This store is what survived my own coaching, nothing I would not strap on a first-timer.

The honest part

I earn from this edit, and I would rather you know.

As a Pilates Addiction creator affiliate I earn 5% of every order here, before tax and shipping. Your 10% with LEX10 comes off your side, never added to your price. My interest is you actually showing up, so I only sell what earns its place in the kit.
