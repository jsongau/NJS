Paid media and cost to acquire

Per unit

Paid media to Amazon sellers and DTC operators. The budget is the monthly spend times the store count times the campaign length, so it scales with the rollout rather than sitting as a lump sum.

Activation, renewal and the seats that never switch on

Aging

A software term is recognized whether or not the seller logs in, so an unused seat is not free margin the way gift-card accounting is. It produces the same year-one revenue and almost no chance of a renewal, which is why activation is the number the sales team is measured on. Unused value is therefore an obligation that stays on the books, not money the business has won.

Official rules summary

This is a concept work sample. The summary below shows the structure a real sweepstakes needs, and would require legal review before any launch.

Sponsor
Would be named here, with mailing address.
Eligibility
Legal residents of California and Arizona, 18 years or older. Employees of the sponsor and their immediate households are excluded.
No purchase necessary
A purchase does not improve the chance of winning. A free mail-in entry method would be published here.
Entry period
Fixed start and end date and time, stated in a single time zone.
Prize
Three year of Rel.ais are awarded each monthly drawing period. Approximate retail value $200 each, $600 per period. Winners are responsible for any applicable taxes.
Odds
Depend on the number of eligible entries received.
Winner selection
Three winners drawn at random from all eligible entries each period using an auditable process, with an alternate-winner procedure.
Notification
By email within a stated number of days, with a claim deadline and publicity release.
Referral entries
One additional entry for the first verified referral who completes an entry. Duplicate, automated, or invalid entries are void.
Privacy
Email and SMS permission are collected separately. Data use, retention, and opt-out are disclosed at the point of entry.
Platform disclosure
The promotion is not sponsored, endorsed, or administered by any social platform.
