# The Brea posting, transcribed verbatim. Source of truth for this wave.

Sales Manager, Main Event Entertainment (part of Dave and Buster's), Brea CA.
$75,662.29 to $89,014.40 a year, full time. Read from the uploaded Indeed PDF on 14 August 2026.

## Opening paragraph

> THE EVENT SALES MANAGER: drives event revenue through consultative selling, outbound
> prospecting, and **retaining an existing client base**. This position requires a big-picture
> thinker who is strategic in **customer acquisition and retention**. To hit the ground running, you
> will need a successful record of increasing sales, cold calling expertise, strong **client
> nurturing** skills, great multi-tasking abilities, and the drive to succeed. Bottom line: Your
> nickname should be "The Closer." This role will work on-site at the location and will report
> directly to the General Manager.

## What we are looking for

W1. You love selling for a fast-paced, multi-faceted restaurant/entertainment scene
W2. You exhibit superior prospecting, presentation, and closing skills
W3. You are skilled in leadership, relationship selling, and new business development
W4. You understand the importance of maintaining customer relationships to earn year-over-year business
W5. You can develop a sales campaign focused on communicating and nurturing customer relationships and driving repeat sales
W6. You are goal-oriented, self-motivated, and self-directed
W7. You like to surpass targets and are driven by your bonus

## Requirements

R1. 21+ years of age
R2. 5+ years of related sales experience
R3. Outgoing and personable; excellent verbal and written communication skills
R4. Ability to travel 10% of the time within the community and to tradeshows or conferences
R5. Strong computer skills, particularly with Excel, Word, Microsoft Office, and CRM Applications
R6. Well-developed presentation and negotiation skills
R7. Experience with outbound prospecting and new business development

## What will you be doing daily

D1. Perform outbound lead-generating activities outside the building, including tabling, networking events, and go-sees with prospective **and current** customers
D2. Develop and execute a local and outbound sales strategy to identify high-potential target customer segments and industries that would benefit from our services
D3. Build and manage a high-performing **sales team**, providing mentorship, training, and support to ensure they achieve their individual and team goals
D4. Manage the full sales process cycle through prospecting, calls, and setting appointments
D5. Monitor key performance metrics and **market trends**, adapting our outbound sales strategy and tactics to stay ahead of the **competition**
D6. Track and report progress on **daily, weekly, and monthly** outbound prospecting and sales goals in our CRM
D7. Utilize the **sales data warehouse platform** to leverage regular communication with the client base and track all **correspondence and communications**
D8. Partner closely with your **District Sales/Regional Sales Managers**

## Perks that carry a product implication

P1. Competitive salary plus **sales commission potential**
P2. **Quarterly bonus program**

## Notes

The posting names retention three times in its first six lines: "retaining an existing client base",
"customer acquisition and retention", "client nurturing". W4 and W5 restate it, and D1 says go-sees
happen with **current** customers as well as prospects. It is the single most repeated idea in the
document.

It is also a **team leadership** job. D3 is unambiguous: build and manage a sales team, mentor,
train, and get them to individual and team goals. The salary band and the reporting line to the
General Manager both fit a manager rather than an individual contributor.
