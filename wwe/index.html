The product never stops. The relationship should not either.

WWE makes original content 52 weeks a year, watched in over 1 billion households. What a fan buys today is scattered: a presale here, a merch drop there, a ticket somewhere else. A membership puts the whole relationship in one place, presale head starts, members-only drops, points on everything, and the behind-the-scenes layer between shows. That is the mechanism: fandom this constant deserves one home, not fifty-two separate transactions.
