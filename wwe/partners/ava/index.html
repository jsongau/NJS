Why this store

My cardio classes run on entrance themes. The merch has to keep up.

Every class I teach opens with somebody's music, and you learn a lot about a roster when eighteen people hit their intervals to it. This drop is what survives that room: the tee that breathes, the pins that travel on a gym bag, the posters that turn a plain wall into a card.

Eight pieces from the shared Ring Circle drop, plus the grip socks I made myself.

The honest part

I earn from this store, and I would rather you know.

The Ring Circle pays me 5% of every product order here, before tax and shipping. My code AVA10 takes 10% off for you and comes from the program's side, never added to your price.

Ringside members pay half on everything instead. The member price replaces my code, it does not stack, and I earn nothing when someone joins Ringside. Memberships carry zero commission, by design.
