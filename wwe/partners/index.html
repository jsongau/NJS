The Ring Circle

A creator commerce program concept for Ringside, a fictional WWE fan membership. A small circle of superfan creators, each with a merch storefront in their own voice, honest commission economics on every receipt, and one headline rule: Ringside members pay half price in every store. Their audiences arrive already trusting the person who invited them.

The economics

How the Circle works

Three sides of one arrangement, printed on every receipt so nothing is hidden.

I

The follower

Gets a 10% code on every order, applied automatically, always. And a Ringside member pays half price on every product in every Circle store. The two do not stack, the member price already wins. The best price in the store belongs to the member.

II

The creator

Earns 5% of every product sale before tax and shipping, paid on product only, never on memberships. The Ringside CTA on her checkout carries zero commission by design, so the pitch on her receipt stays clean.

III

WWE

Every checkout advertises the membership with honest math computed from the real cart: join Ringside for $99 a year and this order drops by the printed amount. Membership acquisition rides on creator commerce, and the 50% is the reason a fan on the fence joins.

The Ringside layer

Every checkout sells the membership

Each store prints the member price next to every product, and every checkout computes what that exact cart would cost at 50% off. The receipt does the selling.

For WWE

Creator commerce becomes a membership acquisition channel the brand never had to buy media for. Every fan who checks out sees real cart math carried by someone they already trust: a $99 membership that cuts $57 off one order is not a discount, it is an argument. The fan on the fence joins at the receipt.

The compliance line

Creators earn commissions on product, never on memberships. The Ringside CTA carries zero commission, the 50% rewards the member directly, and no creator is ever paid per signup, which keeps the whole program clean enough for a global brand to put its name on.

The partners

Three storefronts, three voices

Each store is fully built: the Ring Circle merch catalog, member pricing printed on every product, the creator's own product line, and a cart that prints her earnings and the Ringside member math on every order.

A note from the author

Why I built this

I help creators launch fan-commerce storefronts that multiply the value of a WWE membership. The mechanic is purchasing power: every product carries a member price, every checkout computes the savings from the real cart, and a $99 membership that halves the order does its own selling. Every page here is a working build, the funnel, the three stores, the carts, the member math, made to show how I design growth programs, not just screens.
