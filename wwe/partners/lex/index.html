Why I run it like training

I log every set, and I log every card.

Watch nights at my gym run like sessions: warm-up at doors, predictions scored like reps, standings on the whiteboard. Fandom is a discipline stat here, you show up prepared or you do not show up.

This store is the team kit for that program. Nothing on these shelves is decoration, it all gets worn, hung, or scored on a Saturday night.

The honest part

I earn from this store, and I want you to know the number.

The program pays me 5% of every product sale placed here, before tax and shipping. It is printed on your receipt, so nobody has to wonder what my picks are worth to me.

Your LEX10 code takes 10% off and comes out of the program's side, never added to your price. If you are a Ringside member you pay half instead, the member price wins and the two never stack. And I earn exactly nothing when you join Ringside, that one is yours.
