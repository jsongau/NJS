Why our house watches

It started with one kid asking who the good guy was.

Three years later, Friday is the night nobody schedules against. The cousins come over, the deck comes out, the banner goes up, and for two hours our living room is the loudest building in town. It stays TV-PG at our house, first heroes matter.

So the whole night got its own shelf. This store is that shelf, organized.

The honest part

I earn from this store, and I want you to know exactly how much.

The Ring Circle pays me 5% of every product order placed here, calculated before tax and shipping. Your 10% code comes out of the program's side, never added to your price.

And the Ringside membership the checkout keeps mentioning pays me nothing at all. Members get 50% off everything here, that price replaces my code instead of stacking, and I earn on product only, never on memberships. That one is between you and Ringside, on purpose.
