The mechanism

Your enamel is already made of this

Enamel is mostly hydroxyapatite, a calcium phosphate mineral. NJS delivers that same mineral in nano form, small enough to bind to the surface and settle into early weak spots. That is the whole mechanism: it helps re-mineralize early damage, quiets sensitivity by plugging exposed micro-tunnels, and makes teeth look whiter by smoothing the surface so light reflects evenly, no bleach involved.

Published randomized trials have reported 10% nano-hydroxyapatite toothpaste performing comparably to fluoride toothpaste for remineralizing early lesions and reducing sensitivity. Those studies tested nHA as an ingredient, not this concept formula, and nothing here is a claim to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease.

★

Why this is different: most whitening pastes work by bleaching the tooth or sanding off the stain layer. Smoothing the surface with the tooth's own mineral is the one approach that brightens while the enamel gets healthier, not weaker.
