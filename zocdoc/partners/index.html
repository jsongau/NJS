The Smile Circle

A creator commerce program concept for NJS, powered by Zocdoc. A small circle of influencers, each with an oral-care storefront in their own voice, honest commission economics on every receipt, and a Zocdoc booking CTA at every checkout. Their audiences arrive already trusting the person who invited them.

The economics

How the Circle works

Three sides of one arrangement, printed on every receipt so nothing is hidden.

I

The follower

Receives a private 10% preference on every order, applied automatically with the creator's code, and a further 20% off the subscription for booking a dental checkup through Zocdoc. The best price in the store belongs to the person who goes to the dentist.

II

The partner

Earns 5% of every sale before tax and shipping, including every month a subscription stays active, with an annual prepay handing the full year of residuals over on day one. Creators earn on product only, never on appointments, the Zocdoc CTA carries zero commission by design.

III

The brand

NJS gains storefronts it never had to design and audiences it never had to buy. Zocdoc gains its brand on every checkout and a booking CTA in front of every buyer, patient acquisition riding on a CPG marketing budget.

The Zocdoc layer

Every checkout ends at the dentist

Each store carries Zocdoc branding from the nav bar to the receipt, and every checkout closes with one CTA: book the checkup that unlocks 20% off.

For Zocdoc

Creator commerce becomes a patient acquisition channel Zocdoc never had to pay for. Every follower who checks out meets a booking CTA carried by someone they already trust, and every booking is verified by the confirmation, not by a promise.

The compliance line

Creators earn commissions on product, never on appointments. The booking discount rewards the patient directly, and no influencer is ever paid per checkup, which keeps the whole program clean enough for a healthcare marketplace to put its name on.

The partners

Three storefronts, three voices

Each store is fully built: the NJS oral-care catalog, subscriptions pushed first, annual prepay with a free Travel Duo and Soft Brush, the creator's own product line, and a cart that prints her earnings and the Zocdoc CTA on every order.

A note from the author

Why I built this

I want to help influencers launch health brands of their own, on rails that make the right thing the easy thing: honest commission economics printed on every receipt, the creator's own product line one shelf over from the catalog, and a Zocdoc booking CTA at every checkout so commerce keeps feeding care. Every page here is a working build, the funnel, the three stores, the carts, the economics, made to show how I design programs, not just screens.
