Why I switched

Lifters log every set, then sand their enamel off with charcoal paste.

I tracked every macro and then scrubbed my teeth with the abrasive equivalent of a deadlift bar. NJS was the first paste that explained its mechanism like a coach would: the tooth's own mineral, rebuilt nightly, no bleach, no burn, no bravado.

So the whole routine moved over. This store is that routine, nothing I do not use.

The honest part

NJS pays me 5% of every order placed here, before tax and shipping. Keep a subscription going and I keep earning a small monthly share, so my incentive is that the routine holds, not that you buy once.

Your 10% discount comes out of NJS's side, never added to your price. The 20% you unlock by booking a checkup on Zocdoc pays me exactly nothing, that one is yours.
