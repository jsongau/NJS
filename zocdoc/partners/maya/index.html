Why I switched

I spent years squinting at labels in the toothpaste aisle.

Whitening that meant bleach. Tingle that meant burn. Claims that dissolved the moment you asked how. NJS was the first tube that told me the mechanism, the same mineral enamel is made of filling in the weak spots, and said plainly what it cannot do.

So the whole family's sink shelf moved over. This store is just that shelf, organized.

The honest part

I earn from this store, and I want you to know exactly how much.

NJS pays me 5% of every order placed here, calculated before tax and shipping. If you keep a subscription going, I keep earning a small monthly share, which means my incentive is that the routine sticks, not that you buy once.

Your 10% discount comes out of NJS's side, never added to your price. And the 20% you unlock by booking a checkup on Zocdoc pays me nothing at all, that one is between you, your dentist, and NJS.
